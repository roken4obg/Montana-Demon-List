Submission Guidelines

1. Video Evidence

All completions require unedited video footage starting from 0% to level complete, with visible endscreen stats.

2. Audio Requirements

Audible clicks/taps or raw audio input are strictly required for Top 20 levels.

3. Allowed Hacks & Modifications

Cosmetic hacks, Geode plugins, and FPS bypass (up to 360 FPS) are allowed. Speedhack, hitbox viewers, and automated bots are strictly forbidden.
